Autofill Helper

Fills the page you're on. Nothing more.

Open a job application form yourself, then click Fill. The extension only reads and fills the fields on that page — it never clicks Apply or Submit for you.

    Saved once, reused on every form. Stored only on this device.

    Save answers to questions that keep repeating ("Why do you want to work here?"). When a form's question is close enough to one you've saved, Fill Page will suggest it — you can edit before it fills.

      All data stays on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
